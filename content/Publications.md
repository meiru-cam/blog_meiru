<div class="pub-info">
<strong>Failure Modes in Multi-Hop QA: The Weakest Link Effect and Recognition Bottleneck</strong>
<div class="pub-authors"><span class="me">Meiru Zhang</span>, Zaiqiao Meng, Nigel Collier</div>
<div class="pub-venue">ACL 2026</div>
<div class="pub-links">
<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12499">PDF</a>
</div>
<details class="pub-abstract">
<summary>Abstract</summary>
<p>Multi-hop QA fails in long contexts due to position bias. We propose Multi-Focus Attention Instruction (MFAI) and evaluate 5 LLMs on MuSiQue and NeoQA, identifying recognition failure as the main bottleneck and proposing the "Weakest Link Effect".</p>
</details>
