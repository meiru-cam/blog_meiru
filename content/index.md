# Meiru Zhang

**PhD, University of Cambridge** | Machine Learning Intern at Epic Games

I have been working on LLM post-training and agents: agentic RL for long-horizon, tool-using agents, environment-grounded evaluation, and making LLMs reliable when they reason over retrieved evidence. I completed my PhD at the University of Cambridge in 2026 under Professor [Nigel Collier](https://sites.google.com/site/nhcollier/) and Dr. [Zaiqiao Meng](https://uk.linkedin.com/in/meng), with a thesis on event-centric AI, from oracle-free event extraction to efficient forecasting. I am a [Gates Cambridge](https://www.gatescambridge.org/biography/18434/) Scholar. I am currently a Machine Learning Intern at Epic Games, post-training a coding agent with reinforcement learning.

**Research Interests:** LLM Post-Training & Agentic RL · Tool-Using Agents · LLM Evaluation · RAG & Long-Context Reliability · Robotics (VLM, VLA)
